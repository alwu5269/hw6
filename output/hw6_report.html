5 Recommendation for the manager

Turn it on for real vendor mail only as a draft assistant, with a buyer reading every result. Do not let it act on its own yet.

  • Why yes: it read every practice and exam document, got all 12 practice documents right with the defenses on, never called the email or payment tools, and it is cheap.
  • Why not unattended: a vendor email with different plain-text and HTML parts fooled it every time (6 of 6 runs). We tuned the defenses on the practice set, so 12/12 does not predict new documents. The trap tools were never actually triggered in testing.
  • Before wider use: make the agent read both parts of an email and refuse when the numbers disagree, keep it without any real send-email or payment ability, and spot check a sample of its answers against the documents.
